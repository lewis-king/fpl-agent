## How to read the synthesis delta

A positive synthesis delta means the best jointly simulated squad containing that player was worse than the published choice after both five- and eight-week audit families were combined. It includes the opportunity cost of the transfers needed to afford the player, captaincy, bench order, autosubs and the same correlated match worlds—not just the player's isolated xP.

The report does not claim an omission can never become wrong. Predicted lineups, official status and markets are refreshed again before the deadline; any changed input invalidates this receipt and forces a new decision run.
