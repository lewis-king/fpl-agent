03 · The engine

Prediction is
only layer one.

The useful object is not a list of player xP values. It is a decision policy: a legal 15, a legal XI, captaincy, bench order and plausible future transfers, evaluated together under the same uncertain match worlds.

I

Forecast

  • 244,425 deadline rows
  • 47 point-in-time features
  • Position-specific XGBoost hurdle models
  • Appearance classes: DNP, 1–59, 60–89, 90+
  • FPL's own xP prohibited
II

Simulate

  • Team goal and player component identities
  • Correlated match outcomes
  • Exact autosubs and captain fallback
  • Raw, anchored and high-goal worlds
  • Untouched selection/holdout seeds
III

Decide

  • £100m, 2/5/5/3, max three per club
  • Open, fixed and rolled-GW2 policies
  • GW1–3, GW1–5 and GW1–8 views
  • Mean and downside-aware regret
  • Transactional evidence gate
In plain English

We ask which team we would regret least.

The model estimates who plays and what they score. The simulator then plays thousands of plausible versions of the fixtures. Squads that only look good under one fragile assumption fall away; legal teams that remain close to the best across several views survive.

Technical form

A hurdle forecast feeding minimax-flavoured regret.

xPᵢ = E[pointsᵢ | plays] × P(plays), with calibrated minute-class probabilitiesR(c) = 0.75 Σₛ wₛ[max Vₛ − Vₛ(c)] + 0.25 maxₛ[max Vₛ − Vₛ(c)]

A central-regret cap prevents a stress-only winner from displacing a materially stronger base squad.

07 · The close call

Why not the
GW1-only leader?

The selected squad is not the maximum central GW1 projection. It is a declared season-opening robustness choice. That trade-off is visible so you can disagree with the risk policy rather than being handed a mysterious “optimal” team.

Selected · R-083607ec8c

3-4-3 · 57.05 published GW1 xP

Five-week robust regret 1.528; eight-week robust regret 0.985. It remains rank one in the historically gated five/eight-week synthesis.

GW1 structure leader · R-0aec115e14

+1.39 GW1 points in the independent holdout

The 95% interval is +1.25 to +1.52. It is a real alternative, but ranks 5 in the multiweek synthesis and carries +0.258 more synthesis regret. A newly generated squad without that deep multiweek score would block publication.

Eight-week challenger · R-1ca68907f5

4-4-2 · +1.03 weighted points over eight weeks

It wins the central long-horizon view and a separate exhaustive baseline role/configuration replay (+0.95 residual; +1.18 component). It has materially worse five-week high-goal/raw-component regret, however. The declared synthesis retains the lower combined-regret squad and marks the disagreement for live role verification rather than treating it as settled.

Fresh bench/allocation selector · R-1ca68907f5

A fresh rank-two result, not a material defeat.

The targeted winner beat the active squad by only 0.054 composite points, inside the 0.25 practical-equivalence bound. The active squad retained the lower worst-family regret (0.974 vs 1.238) and won the original selector. The evidence therefore supports a practical tie resolved toward the less brittle squad—not a general rule to spend heavily on the bench.

Immediate role watch · 3 selected players flagged

Ollie Watkins is the most important deadline verification.

Official status is available and the model gives 89% play probability, but he appeared in 0/7 eligible preseason lineups and 2 captured sources currently flag him as bench. Those sources have zero production weight; the scenario audit shows the counterfactual rather than silently overriding the model.

08 · Frontier

What would make
this genuinely SOTA.

The gaps below are ordered by information value. None should be patched with an unvalidated scraper or an opinion-weight chosen because it changes the team.

01

Independent player-event data

Reduces dependence on one event-data ontology and improves chance/role signals.

Two live sources, stable player mapping, point-in-time folds, and legal-policy lift.
02

Matched external benchmark

Internal baselines cannot establish a state-of-the-art claim.

Reproduce OpenFPL or another strong public model on identical folds and decisions.
03

Prospective 2026/27 scoring

New BPS/defensive-contribution rules and live behaviours need untouched outcomes.

Six events for diagnosis; a materially larger sample before any SOTA claim.
04

Predicted-lineup calibration

Watkins-like role uncertainty is currently modelled conservatively but not source-authoritative.

Two-source consensus must beat the champion prospectively on role and legal decisions.
05

Full distribution calibration

Mean xP and policy replay do not alone prove that p10/p90/CVaR tails are calibrated.

Add time-forward CRPS, interval coverage and captain/bench tail calibration by position.
06

Future price and liquidity scenarios

The horizon models points and transfer hits, but not probabilistic price moves or forced affordability loss.

Replay timestamp-safe price forecasts and promote only on net points, not team value alone.
